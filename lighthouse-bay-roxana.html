Chapter One

Getting Here

Approaching from the West
Baltimore · DC · Annapolis
3 – 5+ hours · Arrive from the south
1
Take US-50 East toward Annapolis
2
Cross the Chesapeake Bay Bridge — toll eastbound (EZ-Pass or pay-by-plate)
3
Continue ~115 miles east on US-50: Kent Island → Easton → Cambridge → Salisbury
4
Near Berlin / West OC, exit onto MD-90 East (Ocean City Expressway) — saves 30–60+ min in summer. Only 2 traffic lights.
5
MD-90 ends at Coastal Highway (MD-528) at 62nd Street
6
Turn LEFT (north) on Coastal Highway
7
Drive ~4 miles north to 144th Street — turn and you've arrived
⏰
Bay Bridge timing: Heavy backups Friday 2–7 PM and Saturday 8 AM–noon in summer. Leave before 10 AM Friday or after 7 PM to avoid the worst of it.
Final-mile landmarks
  • Northside Park — bay side, around 125th St
  • Fenwick Inn — around 138th St
  • "Welcome to Maryland" sign — if you cross it heading south, you've gone 2 blocks too far
Approaching from the North
New York · New Jersey · Philadelphia
Philly ~3 hrs · NJ ~3.5–4 hrs · NYC ~4.5 hrs · Arrive from the north
1
Head south on I-95 (or NJ Turnpike → I-95) toward Wilmington, DE
2
Cross the Delaware Memorial Bridge into Delaware — $5 toll westbound only
3
Take DE-1 South (Delaware's Coastal Route) — this is your road for the next ~90 miles
4
Continue through Dover → Rehoboth Beach → Bethany Beach → Fenwick Island
5
DE-1 becomes Coastal Highway (MD-528) as you cross into Maryland — watch for the "Welcome to Maryland" sign
6
Just 2 blocks past the state line — turn right at 144th Street and you've arrived
🌅
Summer DE-1 traffic: Friday afternoons (2–7 PM) get heavy between Lewes and Fenwick. Leave Friday morning or push through to Saturday early. The Bay Bridge crowd doesn't affect this route — that's the silver lining.
Final-mile landmarks (heading south)
  • Fenwick Island State Park — last Delaware stretch
  • "Welcome to Maryland" sign — the state line
  • 146th Street / Beach Liquors — you're one block away
  • 144th Street — turn right (bay side) for the property

🅿️ Parking, once you're here

  • Private garage — code 258789, fits 2 cars
  • Guest parking lot — additional spaces nearby
  • Free street parking — Sinepuxent Avenue (side street)

  • Bag and remove all trash from every floor — kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, loft
  • Trash bins are outside near the garage — please don't leave bags inside

🍽️ Kitchen

  • Rinse dishes and load the dishwasher — run it before you leave
  • Remove leftover food from the fridge and discard
  • Wipe down the stovetop and counters

🏊 Pool fobs

  • Return both pool fobs to the drawer next to the refrigerator for the next guests
  • ⚠️ Missing or unreturned fobs are subject to a $100 replacement fee per fob

🛏️ Bedrooms & linens

  • Strip all beds and leave sheets in a pile on the mattress
  • Drop used towels on the bathroom floor — no need to fold
  • Check under beds and in drawers for personal items

💡 Lights, fans & AC

  • Turn off all lights on every floor
  • Turn off all ceiling fans
  • Set thermostat to 75°F — do not turn it off completely
  • Close all windows before leaving

🏖️ Beach gear

  • Return all beach gear to the garage — 1 umbrella, 2 chairs, 2 surfboards, 1 trolley

📺 Electronics

  • Turn off all TVs
  • Log out of any personal streaming accounts (Netflix, Hulu, etc.)

🔒 Lock up

  • Close and lock all exterior doors and windows
  • Pull the front door shut until you hear the lock engage
  • Lower the garage door and confirm it's fully closed before leaving
